# DevilFruitTCG.gg

DevilFruitTCG.gg is a free all-in-one One Piece TCG platform built for players and collectors who want one place for deck building, collection tracking, live market pricing, matchup analytics, and meta reports.

## What's Live Now

- Home
- Market and card detail pages
- Deck Builder
- Decks
- Collection
- Meta reports
- Matchups
- Player profiles
- About
- Account and auth flows

## Production Deploy

Production deploys from the `main` branch and auto-deploys through Vercel.

## Current Site Identity

The active DevilFruit identity is warm parchment, navy, and gold, with Pirata One headers and a premium One Piece fan-made aesthetic. The older cool-blue, glassy SaaS-style identity is deprecated and should not be reintroduced.
