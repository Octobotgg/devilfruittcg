# One Piece + OPTCG Understanding

This doc exists so an LLM can understand the world it is building for before it starts making product decisions.

This is not a lore encyclopedia. It is a practical understanding guide.

## Why This Matters

DevilFruit should not feel like generic software painted with pirate colors.

An LLM working on this project should understand:

- the soul of One Piece
- how One Piece Card Game players think
- how collectors think
- how those worlds overlap

## The Soul Of One Piece

The most important themes to preserve are:

- **Adventure / Discovery**
  The feeling of setting out, finding something real, and being drawn forward by wonder.
- **Inherited Will**
  The sense that dreams, meaning, and purpose carry forward through people.
- **Freedom**
  Not just rebellion for its own sake, but the feeling of living expansively and honestly.

Other useful supporting themes:

- chosen family
- laughter and joy after hardship
- seriousness beneath the fun
- resisting dead systems and lifeless authority

## What This Means For DevilFruit

The site should feel:

- alive, not corporate
- clear, not cluttered
- premium, not pretentious
- emotionally resonant, not melodramatic
- faithful to the spirit of One Piece, not overloaded with references

Use One Piece language and references with discipline.

Good:

- strong atmosphere
- clear but memorable naming
- tasteful references
- a sense of world and discovery

Bad:

- generic SaaS tone
- constant pirate jokes
- deep-lore dependence
- spoilers or fanfic energy
- filler copy

## How To Understand OPTCG

Do not treat the One Piece Card Game like a spreadsheet first.

Understand it through how people actually play and care about it:

- leaders and colors
- archetypes
- staples and tech cards
- matchup dynamics
- why certain cards matter
- how decks are built and tuned
- how collectors value print treatments differently from players

## Player Value vs Collector Value

These are not the same thing.

Player value can come from:

- meta relevance
- consistency
- matchup role
- deck fit

Collector value can come from:

- rarity
- premium treatment
- iconic characters
- set significance
- scarcity and desirability

Some cards matter for both reasons. Some matter for only one. The LLM should know the difference.

## What To Prioritize In Product Decisions

When making product decisions:

- trust beats fake completeness
- player usefulness beats shallow feature breadth
- clarity beats cleverness
- premium feel should come from quality, not ornament
- data should be understandable, not just present

## Research Rule

If the task touches:

- matchups
- deck builder logic
- market labels
- rarity/treatment naming
- collection meaning
- competitive language

and confidence is low, research first.

Learn enough to act intelligently before making product decisions.
